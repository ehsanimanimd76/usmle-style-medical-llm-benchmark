Answer: B. Laparoscopic appendectomy after perioperative antibiotics

Reasoning: The patient's symptoms (migrating periumbilical pain to the right lower quadrant, fever, leukocytosis, and localized tenderness with guarding) are classic for acute appendicitis. This is a surgical emergency requiring prompt intervention. Colonoscopy and barium enema are not appropriate for acute appendicitis. Discharge with analgesics is inappropriate, and elective cholecystectomy is unrelated. Laparoscopic appendectomy is the standard treatment for uncomplicated appendicitis, with perioperative antibiotics to reduce postoperative infection risk.
