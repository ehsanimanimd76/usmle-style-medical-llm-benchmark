Answer: A. Aggressive intravenous fluid resuscitation and early oral feeding as tolerated

Reasoning: Acute pancreatitis management prioritizes supportive care to prevent hypovolemia, organ failure, and infection. Intravenous fluid resuscitation is critical to maintain perfusion and correct hypovolemia. Early oral feeding as tolerated is encouraged once the patient is stable. Antibiotics are not routinely used unless there is evidence of infection. Exploratory laparotomy and ERCP are reserved for specific complications, and total parenteral nutrition is avoided unless oral or enteral intake is not possible. The cornerstone of initial management is IV fluid resuscitation and early oral intake as tolerated.
