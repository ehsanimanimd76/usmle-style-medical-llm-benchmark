# Scoring Rubric

Each response receives 0–2 points in four domains. Maximum score per question is 8.

| Domain | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Incorrect answer or diagnosis | Partly correct or clinically important error | Correct answer/diagnosis |
| Completeness | Misses the central issue | Main answer correct but important omissions | Adequate coverage of the question |
| Safety | Potentially dangerous recommendation | Generally safe but important qualification omitted | Safe, appropriately qualified response |
| Clinical reasoning | Incorrect or contradictory reasoning | Plausible but incomplete reasoning | Logical, clinically appropriate reasoning |

## Critical error

Mark `YES` for a potentially harmful treatment, major medication/dose error, failure to recognize an emergency, major contraindication, or dangerous diagnosis.

The final clinical judgment belongs to the physician reviewer.
