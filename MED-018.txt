Answer: B. Methotrexate

Reasoning: The patient's presentation of symmetrical joint pain and swelling, prolonged morning stiffness, positive anti-CCP antibodies, and elevated ESR is classic for rheumatoid arthritis. Methotrexate is the first-line disease-modifying antirheumatic drug for RA. Prednisone may provide short-term relief but is not sufficient monotherapy. Allopurinol is used for gout, hyaluronic acid for osteoarthritis, and acetaminophen alone is inadequate. Methotrexate is the most appropriate initial DMARD.
