Answer: A. Intravenous hydrocortisone and isotonic saline

Reasoning: The patient's presentation is classic for adrenal insufficiency and adrenal crisis, with hypotension, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hypoglycemia, low serum cortisol, and diffuse skin hyperpigmentation. Immediate treatment requires corticosteroid replacement with hydrocortisone and volume resuscitation with isotonic saline to correct hypotension and hypovolemia. Levothyroxine, insulin with dextrose, fluid restriction, sodium tablets, and furosemide are not appropriate primary treatments. Intravenous hydrocortisone and isotonic saline are the standard immediate treatments.
