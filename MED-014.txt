Answer: A. Intravenous loop diuretic therapy

Reasoning: The patient presents with acute decompensated heart failure with pulmonary and systemic congestion. Key findings include elevated jugular venous pressure, bilateral lung crackles, S3 gallop, orthopnea, dyspnea, edema, and hypoxemia. Intravenous loop diuretics such as furosemide rapidly reduce volume overload and relieve pulmonary congestion. Intravenous fluid would worsen pulmonary edema. CABG is not indicated without acute coronary syndrome. Propranolol is not initial therapy for acute decompensation. Sodium bicarbonate is irrelevant. Thus, IV loop diuretics are the most appropriate initial treatment.
